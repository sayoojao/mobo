03

Work continues when the network doesn't

The official app has no interface at all without a reachable server, because the UI itself is a remote resource.

Mobo caches the session and the data it needs, states its connection status honestly, and processes POS sales online or offline.

Loading docks, basements, rural sites: the difference between a usable tool and a stalled one.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps

1Install the Mobo connector moduleOn your Odoo server: self-hosted, Odoo.sh or a private server.
2Get the app and enter your server URLFree on Google Play and the App Store, for phones and tablets.
3Sign in with your Odoo loginYour existing username and password. No new accounts, no separate user management.
Your data never passes through Cybrosys
